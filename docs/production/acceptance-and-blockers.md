## Remaining blockers before Google Play submission

1. **Production service configuration:** provide an owned HTTPS backend/domain, production capacity, TLS/gateway limits, source-age/error monitoring, immutable deployment/rollback and log retention. Build that URL into the candidate. The development artifacts deliberately contain no assumed production endpoint, so new searches need configuration; exact local cached journeys remain usable.
2. **Source/data rights and coverage:** resolve provider access/redistribution and imagery/survey rights in source-access-conditions.md, including City/ASPIRE conditions. Verify actual intermodal connections, corrected timetables, transfer geography and fare coverage. Prefer an approved maintained official feed when supplied.
3. **Publisher and Play configuration:** verify package ownership, upload key/Play App Signing, developer contact, public final privacy policy, Data Safety, permission/foreground-service/Health Connect declarations, app access, content rating and account-specific testing requirements. A matching development certificate is not a Play upload-signing approval.
4. **Physical end-to-end acceptance:** install over the user's real prior APK without uninstalling, confirm preserved records, exercise permission denial/revocation, slow/offline networks and map address selection, actual reminders/TTS and Samsung notifications. Use configured optional accounts to verify sharing expiry/stop/revocation and external retention/deletion. Complete internal-track update and pre-launch/crash/ANR checks.

Mixed-operator recurring journeys remain future engineering work and are identified in the UI; current standalone recurring journeys are implemented. A live-vehicle connector would require a supported upstream feed and is future work. Neither capability is simulated.
