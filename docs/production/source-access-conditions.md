# Source access and operational limits

Checked 12 October 2026. Successful public HTTP access is not a redistribution licence or a guaranteed feed. The owner must resolve the listed source conditions before public distribution/deployment. Abeona does not advertise live vehicles from these connectors.

| Source | Verified publication / condition | Runtime use and remaining gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CT Trains community rail website | [Published terms](https://cttrains.co.za/TermsOfUse.php) describe standard schedules, limited delay/cancellation information, possible inaccuracies and the right to change/remove the site | Public-page extraction on the backend and the restored Android rail fallback, with bounded host rate/requests/cache. No integration licence or uptime commitment established; confirm automated access and redistribution with the source |
| MyCiTi official published maps/timetables/fares | Official [route/timetable site](https://www.myciti.org.za/en/timetables/route-stop-station-timetables/) is accessible. Its [official app](https://www.myciti.org.za/en/discover-myciti/using-myciti-on-your-phone/) advertises bus arrival/live updates | Abeona uses published scheduled pages, not the official app's private transport. A current documented reusable GTFS/real-time feed URL, access licence and credentials were not verified in this review |
| City of Cape Town APIs/imagery | Official open-data [MyCiTi route layer](https://citymaps.capetown.gov.za/agsext/rest/services/Theme_Based/Open_Data_Service/FeatureServer/95) and [stop layer](https://citymaps.capetown.gov.za/agsext/rest/services/Theme_Based/Open_Data_Service/FeatureServer/96) exist. The City's general [terms](https://www.capetown.gov.za/general/terms-of-use/) restrict redistribution/commercial use without permission, except specified personal-use cases | These layers are geometry candidates, not verified timetable/live feeds. Confirm the dataset-specific licence and coverage before replacing the working official MyCiTi page data or distributing City imagery |
| Golden Arrow official timetable PDFs/fares | Published [timetable catalogue](https://www.gabs.co.za/Timetable.aspx), fare pages and source documents are accessible | Backend ASP.NET catalogue plus bounded PDF parsing. No general redistribution licence/API access agreement established. Confirm source conditions and how corrected/withdrawn documents are handled |
| Western Cape Golden Arrow GIS survey | Official [stop layer](https://gis.westerncape.gov.za/server2/rest/services/SpatialDataWarehouse/Transportation/MapServer/6) identifies on-board survey capture by ASPIRE, copyright ASPIRE, maximum 2,000 records per query | Group/page/sample within limits. Capture date and current service membership are absent. Verify reuse rights for the bundled and refreshed geometry; do not treat surveyed points as current timed stops |

Official current-feed discovery was checked against provider/City sources; old GIS metadata references 2018 GTFS inputs, which do not establish a usable current feed. This is a limited verification, not a claim that no feeds exist. Prefer a licensed, maintained official feed when one is supplied and its calendars, stop IDs, fares, transfer semantics and timestamps pass the same contract fixtures.

Photon, walking routing, map styles/tiles, imagery, Matrix homeservers, Google identity and any WhatsApp gateway have independent access/privacy/capacity conditions. Their public/demo endpoints are not a production service-level agreement. Verify owned capacity or approved provider usage, attribution, retention and deletion behavior before enabling them in the final distributed configuration. Optional integrations remain disabled unless explicitly configured by a user.
