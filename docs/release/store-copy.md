# Store listing draft

Name: **Abeona**

Short description (under 80 characters):

Plan Cape Town journeys, save places and follow clear travel directions.

Full description:

Make everyday travel easier with Abeona, an independent journey planner for Cape Town.

Choose where you want to go, compare published routes and follow your journey step by step. Nearby stations are suggestions: you can choose another station and remember the one that works best for you.

• Save Home, Work, School and your own places, with an address and preferred station.
• Compare available published rail and bus routes from supported sources.
• Save recurring journeys with the days and reminders you choose.
• Review and share an itinerary through Android's share sheet.
• Follow local journey guidance, with optional spoken instructions and an ongoing notification.
• Choose a calm light or dark appearance.
• Use matching saved timetables when a source cannot be reached, with their original check times shown.

Abeona uses scheduled transport information. It does not provide verified live vehicle tracking. Source coverage, timetable access, holiday services, stop information and fares can vary. Golden Arrow surveyed map stops do not establish current services; some published timetable points need your confirmation. Confirm service and platform changes before travelling.

Location is optional for searching. Active directions use location only during an explicitly started session. Live location sharing is separate, time limited and easy to stop, using an optional Matrix account. Accounts and community features are optional.

Abeona is not affiliated with PRASA/Metrorail, CT Trains, MyCiTi or Golden Arrow.
