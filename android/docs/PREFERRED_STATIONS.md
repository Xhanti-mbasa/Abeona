# Places and preferred train stations · 1.8.1

In **Plan**, choose a saved place or search an address under **From**. The
station selector lists mapped stations from closest to furthest. Choose the
station you actually use: a Work address near Woodstock can use Salt River.
The distances in this list show straight-line proximity, not walking distance.

Leave **Remember station for Work** enabled to keep the choice with that spot.
You can change **Board at** for any journey. Disable the checkbox to make a
one-journey change. **Auto · find nearby usable stations** removes the selected
preference and restores automatic candidate discovery.

Choose **Unibell** under **To**, then select **Home** under **After Unibell**.
The arrival station stays Unibell and Home becomes the final walking endpoint.
**Choose final address or saved place** also accepts another address. If you
choose Home directly under To, **Get off at** lets you choose its arrival
station. **End at the station instead** removes the final walk.

An address or current location can be saved as Home, Work, Campus, Friend or a
custom place directly from Plan. Existing spots, photos, calendars, time
selectors and reminders remain available. Reversing From and To swaps both
places and the selected boarding/arrival stations.

Enable walking estimates to fetch and cache mapped foot paths. Train schedule
queries, map traces and walking calculations use your chosen stations. If a
chosen station is unavailable or outside the walking limit, the planner explains
that and keeps the choice. It does not silently replace it with a closer one.
Train station preferences do not constrain Golden Arrow or MyCiTi discovery.

Station preferences stay on this device and are included in encrypted profile
backups. Old spots/backups remain readable with no station preference set.
